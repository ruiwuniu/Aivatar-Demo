# Fishing SFX Samples

These 12 stereo WAV files are original procedural sound-design samples for
Aivatar's park fishing sequence. They were generated at 48 kHz / 16-bit with
`scripts/generate-fishing-sfx-samples.mjs`.

The generator deliberately refuses to overwrite an existing sample.

## Selected production mapping

- Cast: `fishing-cast-c-line-and-plop.wav` -> `/audio/fishing-cast.wav`
- Bite: `fishing-bite-a-bobber-dip.wav` -> `/audio/fishing-bite.wav`
- Reel: `fishing-reel-b-line-tension.wav` -> `/audio/fishing-reel.wav`
- Display: `fishing-display-c-pixel-fanfare.wav` -> `/audio/fishing-display.wav`

## Casting

- `fishing-cast-a-natural-swish.wav`: soft, natural rod-and-line swish.
- `fishing-cast-b-pixel-swoop.wav`: compact retro-game swoop.
- `fishing-cast-c-line-and-plop.wav`: swish followed by a bobber landing in water.

## Fish bite

- `fishing-bite-a-bobber-dip.wav`: restrained water dip.
- `fishing-bite-b-double-tug.wav`: two tactile line tugs.
- `fishing-bite-c-pixel-alert.wav`: clear retro-game bite alert.

## Reeling

- `fishing-reel-a-ratchet.wav`: dry reel ratchet with an accelerating crank.
- `fishing-reel-b-line-tension.wav`: reel clicks, taut line, and light water detail.
- `fishing-reel-c-pixel-crank.wav`: ascending retro-game crank pattern.

## Displaying the fish

- `fishing-display-a-fish-flop-and-glint.wav`: wet fish movement plus a short glint.
- `fishing-display-b-sparkle.wav`: gentle celebratory sparkle.
- `fishing-display-c-pixel-fanfare.wav`: short retro-game catch fanfare.
