# Aivatar 0.5.2

Cast a line together, discover rare trophies, and turn pond weed into a fresh salad.

## What's new

- **Join your companion's fishing trip.** A speech bubble can invite you to participate. Accept, wait for a bite, then land three timed hits before two misses. The compact fishing-rod QTE uses a soft pixel textile style and varies its timing and difficulty between rounds.
- **Rare fishing trophies.** Successful catches can yield bronze fish, silver wave, golden koi, or starlight crystal trophies. Place them on the room floor or furniture, store them, or sell them. Each trophy is unique per save while owned; only selling it makes it eligible to drop again. Resetting the room layout returns trophies to inventory.
- **Pond weed salad.** Pond weed caught while fishing goes into the fridge. Click it or use the stove's right-click menu to prepare one salad from one weed in six seconds without lighting the stove. Companions can also prepare it autonomously. Eating a serving adds 12 hunger and 3 mood, with new preparation and eating animations for all seven appearances.
- **More rainy park days.** Each calendar week has four seeded rainy days with 6–18 hours of rain each, totaling 48 hours. Weather follows real time and remains consistent after reopening.
- **New pixel assets.** Four trophy decorations, pond weed art, and six matching inventory icons bring the new catches and recipe into the room and park.

## 更新内容

- **一起钓鱼。** 角色抛竿后有机会用气泡邀请你参与。接受后等待咬钩，在两次失误前完成三次时机判定即可收获。钓竿式 QTE 使用柔和的像素织物风格，时机和难度会随机变化。
- **稀有钓鱼奖杯。** 成功收竿有机会获得铜鱼、银浪、金色锦鲤或星光水晶奖杯，可在房间地面或家具上摆放、收纳、出售。每个存档每款持有期间唯一，只有出售后才有资格再次钓到；重置房间布局会将奖杯收回物品栏。
- **水草沙拉。** 钓到的水草进入冰箱。点击水草或右键炉灶，可用一份水草冷拌六秒制作一份沙拉，无需点火；角色也会自主制作。食用后饱食 +12、心情 +3，七种角色均配有制作和食用动画。
- **公园雨天更多。** 每个自然周按存档种子选择四个雨日，每场 6～18 小时，合计 48 小时；天气随现实时间推进，重开后保持一致。
- **新增像素美术。** 接入四款奖杯装饰、水草美术，以及包含沙拉在内的六个物品小图标。

## Catch probabilities / 掉落概率

These probabilities apply only after a successful landing. Failed, escaped, or cancelled rounds award nothing. Owning a trophy converts its roll to an ordinary fish without increasing other trophies' chances.

概率仅在成功收竿后结算，失败、脱钩或取消不发放奖励。已持有奖杯的命中结果替换为普通鱼，不提高其他奖杯的概率。

| --- | ---: | ---: |

| Ordinary fish / 普通鱼 | 44.85% | 64.85% |
| Bronze fish / 铜鱼奖杯 | 0.100% | 0.100% |
| Silver wave / 银浪奖杯 | 0.035% | 0.035% |

## Updating from 0.5.1 / 从 0.5.1 更新

The official 0.5.1 app checks for updates about eight seconds after startup, once per application process. An already-running app can use **Settings → Check for updates**. After 0.5.2 is published as the latest stable release, download the update and choose **Install and restart**. All open persistent rooms must save successfully before installation proceeds.

0.5.1 正式版在启动约八秒后自动检查一次更新；一直保持运行的应用可通过**设置 → 检查更新**发现新版本。0.5.2 发布为最新正式版后，下载更新并选择**安装并重启**即可。所有已打开的持久化窗口必须成功保存后才会安装。

## Install / 安装

- **macOS:** Universal DMG and update archive for Apple Silicon and Intel.
- **Windows:** x64 NSIS EXE and MSI installers.
- **macOS：** 适用于 Apple Silicon 和 Intel 的 Universal DMG 及更新归档。
- **Windows：** x64 NSIS EXE 和 MSI 安装包。

Updater artifacts carry signatures bound to version 0.5.2. These signatures are separate from operating-system code signing; Apple notarization and trusted macOS/Windows code-signing certificates are not configured. Windows native interaction still needs user testing.

更新包使用绑定 0.5.2 版本号的更新专用签名。该签名与系统代码签名不同；Apple 公证及受信任的 macOS/Windows 代码签名证书尚未配置。Windows 原生交互仍需用户实测。
